Nature's Diversity

Penguins 🐧

In nature and zoos, same-sex penguin couples are frequently observed courting, nesting, and successfully incubating and raising abandoned eggs together.

Source: Biological Exuberance, B. Bagemihl (1999)

Bonobos 🐒

As one of humanity's closest living primate relatives, nearly all individuals in bonobo societies are bisexual. They use same-sex intimacy to resolve conflicts and maintain peace.

Source: Biological Exuberance, B. Bagemihl (1999)

Giraffes 🦒

Intimate behavior between male giraffes is highly frequent. In some populations, over 90% of mounting behaviors occur between males.

Source: Biological Exuberance, B. Bagemihl (1999)

Milestones

1969

Stonewall Riots

The uprising at the Stonewall Inn in New York marked the true awakening of the modern LGBTQ+ rights movement and is the origin of global Pride Month.

1990

WHO Depathologization

On May 17th, the World Health Organization officially removed homosexuality from its list of mental illnesses (now recognized as IDAHOBIT).

2001

First Marriage Equality

The Netherlands formally passed legislation becoming the first country in the world to allow same-sex couples to legally marry.

2019

First in Asia

Taiwan passed a special law for same-sex marriage, becoming the first region in Asia to legalize it.

The Spectrum

Sexual Orientation
+

Refers to who you are attracted to emotionally, romantically, or sexually (e.g., Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Asexual). It's about your internal sense of connection.

Gender Identity
+

Refers to your deeply held internal sense of being male, female, both, or neither (e.g., Transgender, Non-binary, Cisgender). It may or may not align with the sex assigned to you at birth.

Queer
+

An inclusive umbrella term encompassing people who are not heterosexual or cisgender. It can also represent "Questioning" for those exploring their identity.
